About tranSnovo

Two Decades of
Transplant Innovation

Founded in 2004, tranSnovo (健耕医药) is China's pioneering innovative medical enterprise exclusively focused on organ transplantation, committed to improving how transplanted organs are preserved, transported, assessed and monitored.

Our R&D center, established in 2010, has built industry-leading platforms across chemiluminescence, molecular diagnostics, suspension microarray and biological raw material preparation — resulting in over 300 authorized patents and FDA key inspection certifications.

As the only domestic company with original drugs at its core in the organ transplant sector, we serve transplant centers, hospitals and research institutions on six continents.

Products & Solutions

End-to-End Transplant Solutions

From organ retrieval through long-term post-transplant care, our portfolio covers every critical step in the transplant journey.

Organ Preservation
Proprietary preservation solutions that significantly extend ex-vivo organ viability and improve transplant success rates.
Organ Perfusion
Advanced machine perfusion technology for normothermic and hypothermic perfusion to maintain and assess organ function ex vivo.
Drug Concentration Monitoring
High-precision detection of immunosuppressants (tacrolimus, cyclosporin, sirolimus, MPA) for personalized dosing management.
Matching & Antibody Testing
Comprehensive HLA typing and donor-specific antibody (DSA) detection to guide optimal organ-recipient matching decisions.
Post-Transplant Monitoring
Biomarker panels and diagnostic kits for early detection of rejection, infection, and graft dysfunction in transplant patients.
Organ Transport System
Intelligent temperature-controlled transport containers with real-time monitoring to ensure optimal conditions during transit.
